Adoptable Dogs How It Works Apply

An adoptable-dogs page that keeps itself up to date

Keep posting on Instagram exactly the way you do now. Every new “Meet…” post becomes a searchable dog profile on the website, with no extra work for the team.

How it works

You post on Instagram

Same “🐾 Meet …” captions and “✨ The Important Stuff” you already write.

The site checks hourly

It reads new posts and ignores anything that isn’t a dog profile (fundraisers, events, etc.).

Captions become profiles

Name, age, breed, size, gender, foster’s notes, ideal home, and rescue story, plus the photos.

Adopters find their match

They can filter by age, size, and gender, then go straight to your adoption application.

Try it with any caption

Paste a caption from any Labelle post on the left. The right side shows the profile the website would create from it, updating as you type.

What the team would do

Nothing new day to day

  • Keep starting adoptable-dog posts with Meet [name] and the The Important Stuff section.
  • When a dog is adopted, keep adding ‼️ADOPTED‼️ to the start of their caption, like you already do. Within the hour, the dog moves to “Happy tails” automatically.
  • FOSTER NEEDED in a caption adds a “Foster needed” badge and a foster application button.

One-time setup (about 10 minutes)

  • Someone with the Instagram login clicks “Allow” once, giving the website read-only access to posts. It can’t post, comment, or see DMs.
  • Access renews automatically and can be revoked anytime in Instagram settings.
  • Link the page from the link in bio and the website’s Adopt page, or host it at adopt.labellefoundation.org.
